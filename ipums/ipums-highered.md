This resource lets you select specific variables that you want to capture.

### Topics
You can filter the variables by topic.
The topics are: Technical, 
- Technical:
    - Individual identification number
    - Reference ID number
    - Survey Year
    - SESTAT weight
    - Sample identifier
    - Survey identifier
    - Survey cohort
    - Survey mode
    - Sequence number
- Demographic:
    - Age
    - Birth year
    - Gender
    - Minority indicator
    - Rase/ethnicity
    - Place of birth
    - U.S. citizenship status, binary
    - Type of US citizen
    - Type of visa held when first came to US
    - Total number of children
    - number of children under age 2
    - children indicator under age 2
